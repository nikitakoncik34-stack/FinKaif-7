# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Node.js (Express), Vanilla JS (reactive modular SPA), PostgreSQL (pgcrypto), CSS3 (Velvet Slate & Cashmere Jade system, Glassmorphism, CSS Custom Properties), HTML5 Canvas (Aurora ambient mesh), Railway deployment.

## Users

Individuals, modern professionals, and creators seeking high-agency, clear, and dignified personal finance management without tedious accounting or shame.

## Product Purpose

FinKaif («Финансы в кайф») transforms personal finance into an intuitive, visually stunning, and organic operating system. It empowers users to understand their cash flow, protect their capital, eliminate emotional leaks, and reach financial independence (FIRE) through continuous compound growth.

## Positioning

FinKaif is NOT a spreadsheet in a card grid or a generic corporate admin template. It is an ergonomic, human-friendly financial cockpit featuring 2-second natural language input, instant privacy masking, the 50/30/20 payday ritual, and FinKaif Brain 3.0 neural intelligence embedded directly into the financial data.

## Operating Context

- Used daily for micro-spends (coffee, transport, lunch) via 1-tap coins or natural language express input.
- Used on payday for the 50/30/20 allocation ritual ("Pay yourself first").
- Used in public environments with instant Privacy Mode (`P` hotkey / eye toggle).
- Used for strategic planning: FIRE milestones, Runway cushion audits, and interactive compound interest simulations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities:
  * Fast natural language parser (`parseQuickTxInput`) with live visual preview pills.
  * Instant Privacy Mode with tabular numeral masking (`•••• ₽`).
  * Interactive Donut chart, Cashflow Wave, and Burn Rate meter.
  * Category budgets with live progress tracking.
  * Wealth goals with milestones and auto-fill actions.
  * FinKaif Brain 3.0 with mathematical FIRE modeling, leak detection, and interactive action buttons.
  * Interactive Wealth Simulator drawer with sliders and dual progress bars.
- Constraints:
  * No external trackers or invasive trackers.
  * Tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) must be used for all financial figures.
  * Mobile-first responsive touch compliance (minimum 44x44px touch targets).

## Product Principles

1. **Finances in Pleasure ("Финансы в кайф")**: Money management is a tool of freedom and psychological ease, not misery or guilt.
2. **Pay Yourself First (50/30/20)**: Allocate 20% to savings/investments in the first 15 minutes of income receipt.
3. **Data Legibility Over Decoration**: Numbers and trends are the primary heroes; graphs provide instant clarity without decorative clutter.
4. **Instant Input & Discretion**: Ingesting expenses must take under 2 seconds; sensitive balances must be maskable with one keystroke.
